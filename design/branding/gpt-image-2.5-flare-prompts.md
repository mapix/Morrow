## Icon

Use-case: logo-brand / production native application icon.

A distinctive native app icon for Smail, an AI email partner. One folded envelope made from two interlocking ribbon-like surfaces, subtly suggesting an S and a continuous conversation. The shape is calm, precise and readable at 32 pixels, with a strong silhouette and generous breathing room. Warm coral and deep warm ink on a pale cotton grey background, compatible with the existing Coral theme. A restrained tactile material, soft side lighting, crisp clean edges. No writing, letters, sparkles, face, robots, AI badges, gradients of purple and blue, secondary objects, extra outlines or watermark. Full-bleed square opaque canvas; no fake OS frame and no baked iOS rounded outer mask. The icon's silhouette should carry the identity.

CLI size: 1024x1024, quality high, PNG. Inspect silhouette/small-scale clarity before integrating into macOS IconSet and iOS asset catalog.

## Landing visual

Use-case: ads-marketing / real product composition.

A product marketing visual for Smail, a native AI email partner. A quiet warm grey workspace with a single coral folded-paper conversation motif leading towards the real product. Preserve a broad, uncluttered area for actual product screenshots and short editable page copy. The material should evoke correspondence and sustained work, with depth from light and paper surfaces rather than dashboard decorations. No fake metrics, testimonials, logos, robot mascots, watermarks, UI labels or invented text. Keep the scene useful as a background for authentic macOS and iPhone screenshots captured from working versions. The app interface is composited from real screenshots after generation; do not invent a working phone app before the native version is verified.

CLI size: 1536x1024, quality high, PNG. Keep heading/copy as deterministic layout text outside the raster. Produce responsive crops only after composition review.
